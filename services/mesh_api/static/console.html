Honest scope of v0.1. The deeper independence architecture below is roadmap, not present fact — see the spec for full disclosure.

  1. Open code, MIT licensed. Every line of the orchestrator, the five agents, and the signing layer is on GitHub. No proprietary inference.
  2. Open spec, CC-BY-4.0. PCEC v0.1 is published as a draft proposal. Reviewers are welcome to fork, critique, and propose v0.2.
  3. Cryptographic signatures. Every bundle is Ed25519-signed against a published DID. The public key resolves at /.well-known/did.json. Anyone can independently verify.
  4. Public transparency log. Every signed bundle is appended to a Firestore chain, anchored by sha256. The chain head is public at /pcec/v0/chain/head.
  5. Reproducible verification. The five-agent flow is deterministic given the same inputs and corpus. Judges and reviewers can re-run any verification end-to-end.

Roadmap (v0.2+, not present): independent operator, stipend-funded academic vet panel, uniform rate card, Linux Foundation donation. Drafted in PCEC v0.1 spec.
