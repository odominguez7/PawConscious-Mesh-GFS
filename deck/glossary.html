Glossary

A short reference for any term in the deck that may be unfamiliar to a brand owner or a consumer reading it for the first time.

PawConscious-specific names
PawConsciousThe brand and the company
The verifiable claim infrastructure for consumer goods. Starts in pet supplements. Operates the protocol described below.
ACPAgentic Compliance Protocol
Our broader vision and brand layer. The category we are building: a callable protocol that AI shopping agents can rely on for substantiation.
PCEC v0.1Provenance for Commerce Endorsement Claims
Our open spec. Defines the JSON-LD shape of a signed evidence bundle, the resolver endpoint, and the cryptographic signature scheme. MIT licensed, on a Linux Foundation donation path.Think C2PA for product claims instead of images.
PawConscious MeshInternal codename for the agent network
The 5-agent system that produces a signed evidence bundle from a product URL. Used in code paths, not in the deck.
Technical acronyms used in the deck
A2A v0.3Agent-to-Agent protocol
The open communication protocol that lets one AI agent call another. Maintained by the Linux Foundation. Our verify endpoint is exposed as an A2A agent card.
ADK 2.0Agent Development Kit
Google's open-source framework for building AI agents. Our 5 agents are ADK-based.
Ed25519A cryptographic signature scheme
The signing algorithm we use on every evidence bundle. Standard elliptic-curve digital signature. Industry standard for tamper-evident records.
DIDDecentralized Identifier
A W3C-standardized way to identify an entity (a brand, an issuer) using a verifiable web address that nobody else can hijack. Each PawConscious bundle is signed against the brand's own DID.
JSON-LDJSON for Linked Data
A web standard for structured data that machines can parse. Our signed evidence bundle is JSON-LD so any AI agent can read it.
PMIDPubMed Identifier
A unique number assigned to every scientific paper indexed by PubMed (the US National Library of Medicine). We cite real PMIDs as the substrate for every signed claim.
BioMCPBiomedical Model Context Protocol
The MCP server we call to query PubMed in real time. MCP is the open protocol that lets AI models query external data sources.
Regulation, compliance, industry bodies
FTC §25516 CFR §255 — endorsement guides
The federal rule governing health and endorsement claims in advertising. Updated November 2023 to explicitly cover AI-generated endorsements and require substantiation that survives third-party scientific review.
NASCNational Animal Supplement Council
Industry body that runs a quality seal program for pet supplement manufacturing GMP and adverse-event reporting. About 300 member brands. Complementary to PawConscious, not competitive.
AAFCOAssociation of American Feed Control Officials
Sets the public-domain definitions of nutrient adequacy and ingredient names for pet food and supplements. We map claims to AAFCO definitions in our compliance step.
PACERPublic Access to Court Electronic Records
The US federal courts' electronic docket. We monitor PACER to surface every class action filing against a pet brand within 72 hours of filing.
C2PACoalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity
The open standard that Adobe led, governed by the Linux Foundation, that lets images carry a verifiable provenance signature. Our protocol does the equivalent for product claims.
Business and investor terms
ACVAnnual Contract Value
The dollar value a customer commits to per year. Used to compare subscription tiers.
ARRAnnual Recurring Revenue
The yearly run rate of subscription revenue from existing customers.
LTV · CACLifetime value to customer-acquisition-cost ratio
A unit-economics signal. How much revenue a customer generates over their lifetime divided by how much it cost to acquire them. Higher is better.
SMBSmall and medium-sized business
In our context, a DTC pet brand at $1M to $10M in revenue.
DTCDirect-to-consumer
A brand that sells directly to shoppers without going through retailers. Pet supplement DTC brands are our beachhead.
PDPProduct detail page
The product page on an e-commerce site — the page where a shopper sees the bottle, claims, reviews, price, and add-to-cart button.
SERPSearch engine results page
The list of products or links returned when a shopper or agent searches for "dog joint supplement" on a retailer or search engine.
GC · CMO · COO · CEOGeneral counsel · chief marketing officer · chief operating officer · chief executive officer
Standard executive titles. We sell to the General Counsel or CMO at a pet brand; our cofounders are CEO and COO.
TAMTotal addressable market
The full dollar size of a market opportunity if every customer paid us. The iceberg on slide 07 shows our TAM stack from $2.8B pet today to $640B+ at the deepest layer.
