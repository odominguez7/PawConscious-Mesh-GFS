08 · Why PawConsciousThe moat

PawConscious wrote the spec the agentic shelf will call

An open MIT-licensed bundle, an independent vet panel, and a founding team with two prior exits in this segment

The same proof, on the shelf and in the agent
01
We wrote the spec
PCEC v0.1, the open Pet Claim Evidence Certificate, is MIT-licensed on the Linux Foundation track Adobe used to ship C2PA.
02
We run the panel
A stipend-funded vet panel from Tufts, Cornell, and UPenn reviews every claim against PubMed and the FTC §255.3 substantiation rubric. Brands cannot pay the reviewers.
03
We ship the bundle
A cryptographically signed evidence bundle (Ed25519) that any AI agent can call and verify in one HTTP request.
04
We have done this before
Two prior venture-backed exits in adjacent consumer categories, both founders from MIT Sloan. Full bios on slide 04.
