# NASC Quality Seal Program — Public-Side Summary

The National Animal Supplement Council (NASC) operates a quality assurance program for the animal supplement industry. The NASC Quality Seal is awarded to member brands meeting the program's requirements.

## Public-side program requirements

1. **Biennial Independent Third-Party Audit:** NASC members must pass an independent on-site audit every two years covering manufacturing, quality control, recordkeeping, and labeling practices.

2. **Written Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs):** Members must maintain documented SOPs covering all manufacturing and quality processes.

3. **Adverse Event Reporting (AER) System:** Members must operate an AER system to track consumer-reported issues. NASC aggregates AER data across the industry to identify safety signals.

4. **Labeling Compliance:** Members must conform to NASC labeling guidelines, which align with AAFCO Model Regulations and FDA-CVM expectations for non-drug animal products.

5. **Random Product Testing for Label Accuracy:** NASC contracts independent laboratories to test member products for label accuracy (active ingredient quantities, absence of contaminants).

## What NASC does NOT certify

The NASC Quality Seal is a manufacturing-quality and labeling-compliance program. It does NOT certify:

- Clinical efficacy of supplement health claims.
- Substantiation of "supports joint health" or similar structure-function claims.
- Veterinary endorsement validity.
- FTC §255 endorsement substantiation requirements.

Claims-side substantiation is the brand's separate responsibility under FTC §255 (federal trade regulation) and AAFCO PF7 (state feed regulation).

## Industry coverage

NASC reports having approximately 300 audited member brands as of 2026. The animal supplement industry includes thousands of total brands; the NASC seal represents a subset of the most quality-conscious manufacturers.
