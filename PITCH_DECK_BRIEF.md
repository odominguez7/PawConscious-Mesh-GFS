## 0. Mission for the designer

Build a ten asset deck (cover plus nine Underscore agenda slides) that makes the partner read past slide two without scrolling LinkedIn, read past slide four without checking the time, and email the founder before slide nine ends.

Every slide has one job. Every line of copy proves an Underscore founder attribute. Every number cites a public source. Every visual feels precise, scientific, and quietly confident.

This is not a deck about pets. It is a deck about an inheritance. A founder who lived inside a Boston veterinary network for years, who studied the agentic protocol layer at MIT during the most consequential platform shift of the last decade, and who built the verifiable claim infrastructure for consumer goods in eighteen days on Google Cloud while every other operator in the category was still writing pitch decks.

The brand owner reading this deck must feel the warmth of a pet curled at their feet and the rigor of a Nature paper open on the table. Pet owners trust certain materials, certain colors, certain silences. The deck must use those. The brand will not yell. The protocol will.

## 1. Ironclad rules

These are non negotiable. Claude Design must run every caption, label, and chart legend against this list.

1. **No hyphens anywhere.** Copy, titles, chart labels, file names. Replace compound adjectives with spaces, alternative phrasing, or a colon. ("AI callable" not "AI callable" with a dash. "open source" without. "vet formulated" without. "cofounder" if ever needed.)
2. **No tilde.** Write "about" or round the number.
3. **Round numbers in thousands, millions, billions.** $2.8B, $11.5M, 200 brands, 7 verticals. Never $2,769,432,180.
4. **Every data point carries a source badge.** 11 pt uppercase letter forms, tracking plus 80, 35 percent opacity, anchored bottom right. Sources draw from Packaged Facts, APPA, Grand View Research, Mordor Intelligence, US Census, FTC.gov, AAFCO, NASC, Topclassactions, NY AG.
5. **One idea per slide.** If a slide contains two headlines, split it. If it contains none, cut it.
6. **Word budget per non team slide is 25 words maximum.** Twelve to eighteen is the sweet spot.
7. **The headline carries the argument.** A general partner reading only the slide titles in order must finish with the business self evident.
8. **Numbers beat adjectives.** "Massive market" becomes "$640B across seven verticals." "Fast" becomes "ninety seconds to a signed bundle."
9. **Never say "Trustpilot" except once.** As the cautionary tale on slide eight.
10. **Never say "AI" in body copy.** Once, maybe, as "agentic." Otherwise describe what the system does.
11. **Always continuous prose.** No bullet point fragments. If a thought needs three lines, write three lines.
12. **Read every slide out loud.** If it sounds like marketing, rewrite it until it sounds like a vet describing a substantiated claim to a regulator.
13. **No emoji. No stock photos of golden retrievers in baskets.** Real photography of working environments, lab settings, certificate craftsmanship.
14. **No paw prints.** No paw motifs. No heart motifs. The brand is conscious, not cute.

**Rules of thumb.**
- Eighty percent of every slide is Vellum or Slate. Fifteen percent is Linen Gray or Pharmacy Moss as editorial structure. Five percent is Verified Amber. Signal Clay appears on slide two and slide eight only.
- No gradients. Color shifts are sharp, at grid edges.
- No drop shadows, no glows, no glassmorphism.
- Amber must be calibrated on the final display. It should feel like the warmth of a vet exam room lamp, not like a notification badge.

### 2.2 Editorial typography

License the fonts. The deck cost is the licensing cost.

- **Display headlines.** Tiempos Headline Regular, 80 to 110 pt, tracking minus 12, optical kerning on. Reference. The masthead of an editorial journal of veterinary clinical nutrition.
- **Secondary display and pull quotes.** Tiempos Headline Light Italic, 40 to 56 pt. Italic for quotes and protocol commitments only.
- **Hero numbers.** Söhne Breit Mono Buch, 160 to 220 pt, tabular figures on. Reference. The instrument readout on a clinical centrifuge.
- **Subheads and body.** Söhne Buch, 22 to 30 pt, tracking 0, leading 1.4.
- **Captions, axis labels, source badges.** Söhne Mono Buch, 11 to 14 pt, uppercase, tracking plus 60.
- **Minimum type size anywhere on a slide is 18 pt.**

If Tiempos and Söhne are unavailable, substitute Newsreader plus Atlas Grotesk plus JetBrains Mono. Never Roboto, Poppins, Montserrat, Lato, or Open Sans.

### 2.3 Grid and composition

- 1920 by 1080, 16 by 9.
- 12 column grid, 80 px outer margins, 24 px gutters.
- 96 px top and bottom safe zones.
- Every slide uses the same anchor. Slide title in the top left of columns one to seven. Footer source badge in the bottom right of columns ten to twelve. Slide number in the bottom left at 35 percent opacity.
- Every slide ends with at least one visible grid unit of empty space. Empty space is a clinical choice, not a mistake.

### 2.4 Visual motifs

- **The seal.** A circular wax seal pressed into vellum, photographed in raking light from a single angle. Used once on the cover and once on slide nine. Forge Amber on Vellum. Reference. Renaissance manuscript closure marks crossed with notary seals.
- **The certificate watermark.** On Vellum slides only, a one percent opacity watermark of a finely engraved medical certificate border. Never over type.
- **Engineering callouts.** Numbered circles one through five for the five agents, with thin Linen Gray leader lines pointing to nodes on a mesh diagram.
- **Mesh diagram.** A geometric node graph in Slate on Vellum, with three nodes highlighted in Amber. Reference. The protein interaction diagrams in a Nature paper.
- **Clinical photography.** Documentary grayscale of a working veterinary clinical nutrition lab, a clinician reading a substantiation file, a hand annotating a printed product label. Forge Amber wash at 8 percent. No dogs or cats in saturated color. If an animal appears, it appears at rest, in dignified composition, never wearing a costume or a bow.
- **Portraits on the team slide.** Single founder portrait. Duotone Slate and Vellum. Tight crop from upper shoulders to eye line. Eye line at the top third. Subdued lighting from a single source. Reference. The portraiture of a foundation annual report.

### 2.5 Data visualization style

- Chart strokes 3 to 4 px in Slate, Amber accents only on the lead series.
- Axis lines 1 px at 30 percent Linen Gray.
- Axis labels in Söhne Mono Buch 12 pt uppercase.

- Bar charts over pie charts. Stepped lines over smoothed curves.
- Hero numbers above the chart, never inside it.
- No 3D, no bevels, no infographic illustrations.

## 4. Deep market research distilled

Every figure carries a source. Cite on slide.

### 4.1 The pet supplement market sourcing

The US pet supplement category reaches between $2.7 billion and $2.9 billion in retail revenue during 2024 and 2025 according to Packaged Facts industry tracking. The number sits inside a broader US pet industry of $158 billion in 2025 per APPA. Growth across the supplement category compounds at five to seven percent annually depending on the source. Grand View Research projects the broader pet supplement industry will reach four billion in 2030.

### 4.2 The catalyst is the plaintiff bar

Cosequin paid eleven and a half million dollars in 2024 to settle a class action over joint mobility claims the brand could not substantiate. VetriScience GlycoFlex is pending on the same theory. Mid America Pet Food paid five and a half million in 2024 over contamination claims. Morgan and Morgan is now templating a multi brand pet food docket. NY Attorney General James won the Prevagen trial in 2024 against Quincy Bioscience for unsubstantiated memory claims, an opening claim of one hundred sixty five million dollars. Every legal team in the category now knows the playbook.

### 4.3 What plaintiffs actually win

Class action damages average between three million and forty million dollars when the substantiation gap is documented. Discovery costs alone average between four hundred thousand and one and a half million dollars per defendant per case. A brand without a substantiation file invents one under deposition. A brand with a signed PCEC bundle hands one over and goes home at five.

### 4.4 The AI shopping shift is the second tailwind

Amazon Rufus shipped in 2024 to one hundred million monthly active users. OpenAI Operator entered beta in early 2026. Perplexity Shopping launched mid 2026. Gemini Shopping is on the Google Cloud roadmap. By 2028 between fifteen and thirty percent of considered consumer purchases will route through an agentic intermediary according to Gartner's 2026 emerging consensus. An agent that recommends a pet supplement needs a callable trust oracle before it can confidently answer.

### 4.5 The non obvious insights that make partners sit up

Three angles to thread through the copy.

The compliance officer at a thirty million dollar pet brand is not a compliance officer. She is the head of customer service in many cases, the founder in others, sometimes the lawyer's nephew. Every substantiation tool that targeted "the compliance team" failed in this segment because the team does not exist. PawConscious Mesh is built for the brand owner who is one demand letter away from a deposition.

The NASC Quality Seal is real but covers manufacturing and labeling, not clinical efficacy. The roughly three hundred NASC member brands still face plaintiff exposure on every health claim they make. NASC partnership is upside, not threat. We close the white space that NASC explicitly does not cover.

The independence principle is the moat. Trustpilot collapsed credibility by accepting paid placements from the very brands it scored. Yelp faced the same accusations. ACP makes independence structural. Brand rate cards are uniform. The vet panel is academic. The protocol governance is open. A retailer cannot purchase rubric changes. This is the answer to the "what stops you from being captured" question that every Series A partner asks pre meeting.

## 7. Brand identity research

The intersection brief from Omar. Pet, vet, Google, tech, ACP, trust. The brand cannot read as a vet startup, a SaaS startup, a pet influencer, or an enterprise compliance vendor. It reads as a small foundation that happens to operate the trust protocol.

### 7.1 Brand archetype

The brand sits between three archetypes.

The Sage. The brand is wise about claims, evidence, regulators, and the limits of its own knowledge. Wikipedia for trust claims. The vet at a dinner party who quietly corrects the room.

The Caregiver. The brand cares about the dog the founder is curled next to, not the metrics on a dashboard. The protocol exists because pet owners deserve trustworthy claims about the products their family eats every day.

The Magician. The brand makes invisible infrastructure feel inevitable. A wax seal on a digital file. A ninety second multi agent fan out that produces a Ed25519 signature with grounded PubMed citations. A protocol that nobody needs to think about because it always works.

Three archetypes braided. The voice is calm, the type is editorial, the visuals are clinical, the color palette is warm.

### 7.4 The brand wordmark direction

Tiempos Headline Regular set tight, two words on one line, lowercase optical center dot between Paw and Conscious removed for a quiet word break. The Mesh descriptor sits below in Söhne Mono Buch 11 pt uppercase tracking plus 120, half the width of the wordmark, set in Pharmacy Moss.
